CARS training progression

Resource utilization

  • Jack Westin: Jack Westin is widely considered the best, free, non-AAMC resource for daily MCAT CARS practice. Use daily passages for building strategy, endurance, and hitting early accuracy goals.
  • AAMC CARS Question Bank: AAMC CARS logic is unique and practicing it exclusively during the second half of your prep fine-tunes your reasoning to best prepare for Test Day. Transition strictly to official AAMC materials in the final 6-8 weeks.

Your schedule will automatically indicate WHEN to transition from Jack Westin to AAMC Material based on your total prep time.

CARS progression outline

1Phase 1: Acclimation
  • Daily Goal: 1 CARS passage per day
  • Focus: Developing a standard reading/annotating technique (e.g., highlighting main ideas, tracking author tone/attitudes).
  • Time Constraint: Untimed or relaxed timing (11–15 min/passage). Do not rush.
  • Review: Thoroughly analyze both correct and incorrect answers to understand why options are right or wrong.
2Phase 2: Accuracy
  • Goal: Achieve 80%+ accuracy on 5 individual passages.
  • Format: 1-2 passages per day, untimed or relaxed timing
  • Execution: Focus on paragraph summaries, main ideas, and eliminating distractor choices.
3Phase 3: Timing
  • Goal: Achieve 80%+ accuracy on 5 individual passages at 10 minutes per passage.
  • Format: 1-2 timed passages per day, strict 10-minute time limit.
  • Execution: Target ~4-4.5 minutes to read and ~5.5–6 minutes to answer questions per passage.
4Phase 4: Endurance I (3-Passage Blocks)
  • Goal: 80%+ accuracy on 3 consecutive passages at 10 minutes per passage (30 min total).
  • Frequency: 2–3 block sessions per week.
Transition from individual passages to consecutive passage blocks to build stamina
5Phase 5: Endurance II (6-Passage Blocks)
  • Goal: 90%+ accuracy on 6 consecutive passages at 10 minutes/passage (60 min total).
  • Frequency: 2 block sessions per week.
6Phase 6: Full Section
  • Goal: 90%+ accuracy on 9 consecutive passages at 10 minutes/passage (90 min total).
  • Frequency: 1-2 full-length CARS section simulations per week.
7Phase 7: Buffer Pacing
  • Goal: 95%+ accuracy on 9 consecutive passages at 9 minutes per passage (81 min total).
  • Why 9 minutes? Aiming for 9 minutes per passage leaves you with a ~9-minute buffer on test day to review flagged questions or address difficult passages.
  • Execution:
    • Complete full 9-passage blocks.
    • Integrate CARS seamlessly into your full AAMC Full-Length practice exams.
