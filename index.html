Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I fill out the intake survey?

You'll get a link to book your free strategy consult. We'll go over the results of your survey together on the call during which we will assess your starting point, identify your biggest roadblocks, map out your approach, and discuss your next steps.

Is the strategy consult really free?

Yes. There's no obligation to buy anything. If you leave with a clearer plan for your MCAT preparation, that's a win in my book.

Which prep resources do you support?

Content review schedules currently support Kaplan, UWorld and Khan Academy. Anki integration supports the AnKing MCAT deck, MileDown, and Captain Hook. The CARS framework is designed with Jack Westin passages and the new AAMC Question Bank in mind. Practice question scheduling is resource agnostic.

Can I switch from Core to Coaching later?

Yes. Upgrade anytime, and what you paid for Core is credited toward your first month of Coaching.

How far out from my test date should I start?

Earlier is easier, but schedules can be built for most timelines. Put your test date in the survey and we'll talk about what's realistic on the call.
