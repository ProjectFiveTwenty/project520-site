The real problem

Most students don't fail on content. They fail on management.

You can have every book, every Q-bank and every Anki deck and still lose months to a plan that was never built for you. These are the mistakes I see most.

  • 01
    Resource overloadBuying everything and finishing nothing.
  • 02
    Copying someone else's scheduleA 528 scorer's plan was built for their life, not yours.
  • 03
    Starting practice questions too lateContent review alone doesn't build test performance.
  • 04
    Misusing or skipping AnkiUnfocused decks, runaway review counts, or no spaced repetition at all.
  • 05
    Not interleavingStudying topics in isolation instead of mixing the ones that belong together.

Questions

Good to know.

What happens after I fill out the intake survey?

You'll get a diagnostic summary of your prep, then a link to book your free strategy consult. We'll go over the diagnostic together on the call.

Is the strategy consult really free?

Yes. There's no obligation to buy anything. If you leave with a clearer plan and never sign up, that's still a good call.

Which prep resources do you support?

Content review schedules currently support Kaplan, The Princeton Review, UWorld and Khan Academy. Anki integration uses the AnKing MCAT deck.

Can I switch from Core to Coaching later?

Yes. Upgrade anytime, and what you paid for Core is credited toward your first month of Coaching.

How far out from my test date should I start?

Earlier is easier, but schedules can be built for most timelines. Put your test date in the intake survey and we'll talk about what's realistic on the call.
